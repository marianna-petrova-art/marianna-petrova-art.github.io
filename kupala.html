KUPALA

Horror Visual Novel · 2026 · Ren'Py

About the Game

Kupala is an atmospheric horror visual novel inspired by Slavic folklore.

The story follows a family spending their summer in the countryside, where familiar traditions, old legends, and seemingly ordinary places gradually reveal something unsettling.

The game combines hand-drawn black-and-white illustrations, an unsettling atmosphere, and a branching narrative with multiple endings.

My Work

I independently developed Kupala over the course of approximately a year and a half.

I wrote the story and dialogue, created the hand-drawn illustrations, implemented the game in Ren'Py, and prepared it for release on Steam.

The project allowed me to combine my interests in storytelling, illustration, and game development into a complete playable experience.
