KUPALA

Horror Visual Novel · 2026 · Ren'Py

About the Game

Kupala is a complete, nonlinear horror and drama visual novel inspired by Slavic folklore. The game is available in Russian and English.

The story follows 12-year-old Anya, who arrives with her family in a remote village just before Ivan Kupala Night, an ancient Slavic celebration.

The game explores traditional Russian village life and elements of Slavic mythology. Folklore characters and unfamiliar terms are explained in an in-game glossary. The village, the old house, and many everyday details were inspired by the developer's own childhood memories of spending time in the countryside.

Throughout the story, players make choices that directly affect Anya's fate, leading to one of five different endings.

The game features 65 original, hand-drawn illustrations. Its dark atmosphere, sense of unease, and anticipation of something unknown contrast with the warmth and comfort of an old countryside home.

My Work

I developed Kupala entirely on my own over the course of approximately a year and a half, working on the project alongside my university studies and job.

I was responsible for every stage of development, including the initial concept, story development, scriptwriting, character creation, hand-drawn illustrations, programming in Ren'Py, UI design and implementation, music and sound effect selection, playtesting, bug fixing, and preparing game builds.

I also created and designed the game's Steam store page, managed updates, prepared the game for release, and published it myself.

Working on Kupala was an invaluable experience for me. As my first independent game project, it taught me a great deal through hands-on experience. I made mistakes, learned how to fix them, and continuously improved the game throughout development. I'm confident that everything I've learned will help make the development of my next project much smoother.
