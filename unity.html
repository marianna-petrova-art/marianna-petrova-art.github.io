Unity 3D Project

Unity · C# · 3D Game Development

This is a small learning project I created to explore 3D game development in Unity.

I used free 3D assets to build a small environment and focused on making it interactive. As I worked on the project, I gradually learned how to work with the Unity editor, C# scripts, and different gameplay mechanics.

What I Worked On

Gameplay & Environment

Environment & Lighting

I learned how to import and arrange 3D models, decorate rooms, adjust objects and materials, and work with lighting to create the atmosphere I wanted.

Player Controls

I added first-person movement and camera controls, allowing the player to walk around and explore the environment.

Object Interactions

I made it possible to pick up, carry, and drop objects, open doors, drawers, and windows, and interact with different items, including food and kitchen objects.

Interface & Messages

I created on-screen instructions that change depending on the player's actions. For example, when holding an object, the interface shows that pressing E will drop it. I also added contextual messages and character thoughts.

Item Combinations & Timed Actions

I experimented with combining ingredients to prepare a sandwich, as well as timed interactions such as washing hands and eating, with progress bars.

Game Logic & C# Basics

I learned how to use conditions in scripts to control when actions become available. For example, the player must wash their hands before they can eat. I also worked on preventing conflicting interactions.

My Learning Experience

I used ChatGPT to help me understand Unity and C# scripts throughout the project. Over time, I became comfortable navigating the Unity editor, setting up objects and components, adjusting the environment, and making small changes to existing scripts independently.

I'm continuing to learn C# and enjoy experimenting with new gameplay mechanics.
