Blender 3D Models

Blender · 3D Modeling · Texturing

I've been learning how to create 3D models in Blender, mainly focusing on furniture and objects for game environments.

I usually work with references while learning to build models, adjust their shapes and proportions, and create movable parts such as drawers and doors. I also experiment with materials and textures, including creating and editing textures in Krita.

I'm learning how to prepare models for use in game engines such as Unity. Although my main interest is developing my own games, I enjoy 3D modeling and would like to continue improving my skills.
