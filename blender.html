3D Modeling

Blender · Krita · Unity

I have basic experience with Blender, creating 3D furniture and environment props for game projects.

Most of my models are created from scratch using visual references. I can create movable elements, such as drawers and doors, work with materials and textures, and import 3D models into Unity. I also use Krita to create and edit textures.

I enjoy working in Blender and would love to explore 3D modeling further in the future. At the moment, my main focus is on developing my own games and personal projects.
