About Me

I'm an independent game developer and 2D artist with a passion for creating atmospheric stories, characters, and game worlds.

I spent a year and a half independently developing my first game, Kupala, a horror visual novel released on Steam in 2026. I was responsible for the story, illustrations, programming, and preparing the game for release.

I work with Ren'Py, Unity, Blender, Krita, and Adobe Photoshop.

I'm currently working on my own projects, but I'm also open to joining a game studio or collaborating with other creators. I'd love to be part of a team creating atmospheric and memorable games together.

Languages: Russian (Native) · English (B2) · Estonian (B2)
