About Me

I'm an independent game developer and 2D artist with an interest in storytelling, illustration, and game development.

In 2026, I released my first horror visual novel, Kupala, which I developed independently. I worked on the story, illustrations, programming, and visual design.

I primarily work with Krita for digital illustration and am expanding my skills in Unity, Blender, and 3D game development.
